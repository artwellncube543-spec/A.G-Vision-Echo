Core Feature

Navigate Shops &
Streets Confidently

Real-time voice-guided navigation for streets, shops, markets, and public spaces — designed specifically for how visually impaired people experience space.

LIVE MAP — NAIROBI CENTRAL MARKET
Voice Guide: Turn left in 10m — Pharmacy on your right
  • 🗣️

    Voice-First Navigation

    Step-by-step audio directions describe turns, obstacles, shop entrances, and landmarks in natural language — no screen required.

  • 🛒

    In-Shop Guidance

    Navigate store aisles, locate products by description, and get help at checkouts. Works offline in mapped areas.

  • 🤝

    Live Expert Assist

    Connect instantly with a nearby trained guide or remote expert who can describe your surroundings via audio.

  • 📍

    Community-Mapped Routes

    Routes verified and described by blind users themselves — the most accurate, real-world navigation available.
